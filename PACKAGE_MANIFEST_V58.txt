Main feature
- One vehicle record can contain multiple transport jobs.

Multi-job fields per job
- Job/cargo name
- Origin and destination
- Load and unload dates
- Distance
- Loading and unloading weight
- Stone and wood-chip legacy weight fields
- Trip fee, allowance, other income, total income
- Wage payer, payment status, note

Automatic totals
- Job count
- Total distance used for fuel-rate calculation
- Total loading/unloading weight
- Total stone/wood-chip weight
- Total trip fee, allowance, other income and total income

Compatibility
- Existing single-job records are converted at read time into one legacy job.
- No MongoDB migration is required.
- Legacy top-level delivery fields remain available.
